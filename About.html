About Maunga Club

A place for members to stay together on the mountain.

Enjoy the mountain

Maunga Club hires shared ski and snow chalets to club members. All three chalets are on the mountain, so members can ski to and from their accommodation.

The mountain has 550 hectares of skiing and snowboarding area and the longest ski and snowboard season in New Zealand.

Check current weather and mountain safety information before your trip.

Important booking information

  • Only Club members can access the chalets.
  • All chalet bookings must be from Friday to Sunday.
  • The chalets offer shared accommodation for groups of different sizes.
  • Check the adult and child limits before choosing a chalet.
